## Visual language
- **Two-world contrast:** 1) clean, off-white "paper" canvases with tiny isolated objects, and 2) deep black with glowing thermal / warm-light figures.
- **Pixel art as hero objects:** chunky 32–64 px sprites, scaled up with hard edges and a 3/4 isometric tilt. The objects are nostalgic everyday items (record, camera, coin, controller, heart, cat, book, plant, money, cap, skateboard, clapperboard).
- **Hand-made marks:** ink scribbles, red pen loops, dashed construction lines, brush strokes and ink blots used as wipes.
- **Kinetic typography:** a heavy geometric/grotesque sans in lowercase, with type-on reveals, a blinking block cursor, letter scattering and tumbling letters.
- **Optical FX:** an anamorphic 4-point star flare (cyan), soft bloom, vignettes, film grain and subtle chromatic softness, with motion-blur smears on fast moves.
- **Lo-fi capture texture:** slight compression, grain and warm color grading. It feels like an internet scrapbook or zine.

## Palette
- Off-white / warm grey paper `#E6E4E3`–`#D9D6D4`
- Near-black `#0E0C0C` and charcoal `#2B2B2B`
- Signal red `#E0261B` (book background, pen loops, notes, star burst)
- Thermal orange → yellow `#FF5A00` → `#FFB000`
- Electric cyan / blue flare `#19C8FF` → `#1B3BFF`
- Pixel accents: coin gold `#F4C20D`, plant green `#3FA34D`, cash green `#4CAF50`

## Editing rhythm
- Roughly **15 distinct shots in 24 s**, with many more micro-events inside them. Shots last 0.5–3 s.
- Cuts land on audio transients. Fast cuts mix with "object physics" moments (orbit → collide → burst).
- Transitions: hard cuts, ink-blob wipes, flare sweeps, scale punches (huge type → small) and match-cuts by object (the vinyl recurs in three scenes).
- The camera is mostly static and graphic. The motion is in the elements, not the camera.

## Audio
- A spoken **voiceover** (male, intimate, close-mic) carries the narrative. The VO harmonics sit below about 2 kHz.
- A soft, warm music bed underneath: mellow lo-fi/ambient keys with gentle pulses. The onset autocorrelation suggests **~86–90 BPM (or double time, ~172)**.
- Broadband transient **SFX on cuts:** clicks, whoosh/swish, pops, glitch ticks and paper/ink sounds. These show as vertical spectral lines up to 17 kHz at about 1.3, 6.3, 8.0, 9.3, 10.7, 11.7, 15.7, 16.0, 16.6 and 17.0 s.
- A deliberate **dropout to near-silence around 20.5–21 s** before the outro, which makes the logo land.
- Average spectral centroid is about 1.1 kHz: warm, not harsh.

## Recurring motifs to source
Pixel everyday objects · 4-point star flares · ink scribbles and blots · red hand-drawn loops · thermal / heat-map human silhouettes · glowing hands · blinking cursor and typewriter text · scattered letters · paper-white backgrounds · vignette and grain · retro media objects (vinyl, camera, cassette, controller) · social/UI glyphs.

## What to avoid
Generic cinematic drone shots, corporate tech (servers, handshakes, glowing circuit brains), glossy AI renders and brand logos (the reference includes a Nike swoosh and the Instagram mark, so do not reuse trademarks).
